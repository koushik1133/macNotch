Aurora

A slow aurora in SparkV red, magenta and deep blue wakes up behind the island the moment the pointer arrives. It flows around the edge like light leaking past a glass bubble, with a thin moving colour rim and a tinted glass lip on top.

How SparkV will build it

Native technique, energy budget, and accessibility behaviour.

Technique

Glow layer. Behind the black NotchShape sits an AngularGradient (#F01822, magenta, violet, deep blue) slightly larger than the island and blurred with .blur(radius: 15). Its angle turns through withAnimation(.linear(duration: 6).repeatForever(autoreverses: false)). In AppKit this is a CAGradientLayer of type .conic with a CABasicAnimation on its transform. That runs on the render server, so the app's main thread stays asleep.

Rim and glass lip. NotchShape().stroke(AngularGradient(...), lineWidth: 1.3), plus an inner 9 pt stroke at 28% clipped to the shape for the tinted-glass edge. On macOS 26 the lip is .glassEffect(.regular.tint(.sparkRed.opacity(0.25)), in: NotchShape()). On 14 and 15 it falls back to .background(.ultraThinMaterial) masked to the edge.

ZStack {
  AngularGradient(colors: aurora, center: .top, angle: .degrees(phase))
    .frame(width: w + 40, height: h + 36).blur(radius: 15)
    .opacity(isHovering ? 0.95 : 0)
  NotchShape(size: size, bottomRadius: r).fill(.black)
  NotchShape(size: size, bottomRadius: r)
    .stroke(AngularGradient(colors: aurora, center: .center), lineWidth: 1.3)
}
.animation(.easeOut(duration: 0.5), value: isHovering)

GPU and battery

At rest
Zero. The layer is hidden and its animation paused (layer.speed = 0).
Hovering
One gradient and one 15 pt blur pass over about 280 × 80 pt, composited by the window server. No per-frame app code.
Open
Blur area grows to about 620 × 200 pt at 60% opacity. Still one pass.
Low Power Mode
The rotation stops and the colours stay still.

Accessibility

  • Reduce Motion: the aurora and the rim colours stop moving. They only fade in over 200 ms and the drawer cross-fades. This demo does the same.
  • Reduce Transparency: no blurred glow and no glass lip. Only a solid 1.5 pt gradient stroke.
  • Increase Contrast: the rim gets wider (2 pt) and fully opaque.
