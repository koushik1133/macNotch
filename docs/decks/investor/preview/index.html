Solution

Everything lives in one place the user already looks at. Hover the notch and the drawer opens; move away and it closes. Speaker notes

This is not a mock-up. SparkV's own UI Audit controller audited a bundled test site with planted issues on 127.0.0.1: six pages, 69 findings, three of them critical and fifteen high. Apple Intelligence wrote the summary on device, and the P button produced a 51,666-character fix prompt covering 60 issues. Its sibling, Browser Flows, is still in beta: its self-test was not clean on 22 October.

Speaker notes

Three things lined up. The notch arrived on the MacBook Pro in 2021. Since mid-2023 every Mac Apple sells runs Apple silicon. And at WWDC 2025 Apple opened its on-device model to developers; in Craig Federighi's words, it runs without cloud API costs and works offline. That turns AI from a per-request cost into a free capability of the hardware. Competitors built on cloud models have to charge monthly. We do not.
