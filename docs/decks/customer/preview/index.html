06

Talk · Pro (basic dictation is Free)

Talk instead of type.

Hold fn anywhere. Talk. Release.

Dictation removes filler words and formats lists.

Reply Drafter (⌃⌥R) writes a reply in your tone. You press Send.

Prompt Refiner turns a rough ask into a clear AI prompt.

Add your own words so names are spelled right.

Speaker notes

Put the cursor in any text field, hold fn, speak, let go. SparkV cleans up the text and types it in. Double-tap fn for hands-free. In Mail, Slack or Messages, Control-Option-R reads the thread and drafts a reply in your tone; you check it and press Send yourself. Prompt Refiner asks two to five quick questions and gives you a structured prompt for whichever AI tool you use. [Live check required: fn key, speech, Reply Drafter.]

07

Screen help · Pro

Ask about anything on your screen.

Works in any app, on any display.

Hold ⌃⌥ and ask. Clicky answers and points to the spot.

⌃⌥S: select a chart or an error, then ask about it.

⌃⌥T: copy text from images, videos and locked PDFs.

Speaker notes

Stuck in an app you don't know? Hold Control-Option and ask out loud. Clicky looks at the screen under your mouse, answers out loud and in the band under the notch, and flies a pointer to the right spot. It points; it doesn't click for you. Control-Option-S lets you select part of the screen and ask about it. Control-Option-T copies the text out of anything: a screenshot, a video frame, a locked PDF. [Without an optional Groq key, Clicky reads screen text on device. Live check required.]

10

Focus · Pro

Deep work on a schedule.

Focus rounds, one-click Modes and a nudge to sit back.

Focus: 25/5, 50/10 or 90/15 rounds, Do Not Disturb and a recap.

Modes: one click sets apps, wallpaper, volume and music. Turn it off to restore.

Screen Distance: a reminder when you sit closer than 40 cm.

Speaker notes

Pick a rhythm and start Focus. SparkV turns on Do Not Disturb, runs the rounds, nudges you when you open a distracting app and writes a recap. A glowing border marks the start, breaks and the end. Modes switch your whole setup in one click, Study, Meeting, Presentation, and put everything back when you turn them off. Screen Distance is off by default; switch it on and it reminds you to move back from the screen. [Live check required: DND, glow, modes, camera.]

Builders · Pro

Power tools for builders and job seekers.
