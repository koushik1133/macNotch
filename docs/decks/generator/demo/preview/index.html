05

Screen help · Pro

Ask about anything on your screen.

Works in any app, on any display.

Hold ⌃⌥ and ask. Clicky answers and points to the spot.

⌃⌥S: select a chart or an error, then ask about it.

⌃⌥T: copy text from images, videos and locked PDFs.

Speaker notes

Stuck in an app you don't know? Hold Control-Option and ask out loud. Clicky looks at the screen under your mouse, answers out loud and in the band under the notch, and flies a pointer to the right spot. It points; it doesn't click for you. Control-Option-S lets you select part of the screen and ask about it. Control-Option-T copies the text out of anything: a screenshot, a video frame, a locked PDF. [Without an optional Groq key, Clicky reads screen text on device. Live check required.]

06

Meetings · Pro

Meeting notes, no bot in the call.

One click to record. Transcribed on your Mac.

Minutes, decisions and your tasks with due days.

A suggested answer when someone asks you a question.

Live translated captions, on device.

Filed into the right Space automatically.

Speaker notes

Click the red button and SparkV records your microphone and the call's audio. Nothing joins the meeting. If someone asks you a question, a suggested answer appears under the notch. When you stop, you get minutes, decisions, your own tasks with due days, other people's tasks and the full transcript, filed into the right Space. Turn on Live Translation and other people's speech becomes captions in your language, translated on your Mac. [Live check required: audio capture, translation.]
